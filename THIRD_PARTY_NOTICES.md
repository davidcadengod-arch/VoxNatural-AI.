# Third-party inventory for Beta 3

This APK is an internal verification build. Distribution approval is pending. The Android build pins the model archives, Sherpa AAR, LAME archive, Supertonic license, Pocket export and synthetic Alex input. The compliance artifact records the resolved Gradle graph, input and asset SHA-256 manifests, and the ARM64 libraries and their dynamic dependencies.

The remaining binary audit must identify native dependencies folded into the AARs, map each to its precise source and build recipe, complete license texts and notices, and review the model rights listed above. License approval also requires evidence that a replacement phonemizer supports unrestricted text, punctuation, numbers and medical terminology in both target engines on Android. The Linux UNAM experiment generates a lexicon for its sample corpus for Kokoro and sends phoneme code points for Piper.
